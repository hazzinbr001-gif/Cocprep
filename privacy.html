Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Information we collect

COCPrep collects the email address and password credentials you provide when creating an account. We also store practice activity, answers, progress, and flagged questions so the service can keep your preparation history available across devices.

How we use information

We use account and practice information to authenticate you, provide the question bank, calculate progress, and improve the reliability of the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect account and practice data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password and keep it private.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. To request account or data assistance, contact the COCPrep administrator through the support channel provided with your account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The latest version will always be published on this page with its revision date.
